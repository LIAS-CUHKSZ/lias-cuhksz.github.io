**Authors:** Haoying Li, Qihang Liu, Yifan Peng, Keyan Miao and Junfeng Wu.

**Accepted by:** 2026 Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL).

# Abstract

PLATO uses accurate trajectory observations to improve neural inertial odometry when IMU bias and uncertain sensor noise degrade motion estimates. Its likelihood objective jointly trains a neural ODE for time-varying IMU bias and estimates accelerometer and gyroscope noise covariances. The optimization takes advantage of sparsity, using forward differentiation for noise parameters and a double-adjoint method for the coupled bias and preintegration computations to reduce memory requirements. Experiments on EuRoC demonstrate improved estimation performance, while underwater robot trials show the method's applicability when visual sensing deteriorates or lighting is intermittently lost.
